# Synthetic Harbor — credential handling

This document is synthetic test data written for a feasibility spike. Harbor is
not a real organisation, and nothing here describes a real system, account, or
credential.

Effective 2026-01-01, production service credentials must be rotated every 45 days.

The platform team tracks rotation dates and raises a work item one week before
each deadline.

Exceptions require sign-off from the service owner and are recorded in the same
tracker. Staging and development credentials are out of scope for this policy.
